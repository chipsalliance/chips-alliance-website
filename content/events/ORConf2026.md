CHIPS Alliance is proud to support ORConf 2026, the Mystic Lamb Edition, as a Silver Sponsor. Hosted by the FOSSi Foundation, the 12th installment of ORConf will be held September 11–13 in Ghent, Belgium.

ORConf brings together the open source silicon community for a weekend of presentations and networking focused on free and open source silicon. [Learn more >>](https://fossi-foundation.org/orconf/2026)
