CHIPS Alliance was proud to be a Silver Sponsor of FOSSi Foundation’s ORConf 2026, held September 11–13 in Ghent, Belgium.

ORConf is a weekend of presentations and networking dedicated to free and open source silicon. CHIPS Alliance Platinum Member Antmicro presented several CHIPS Alliance-related sessions covering SystemVerilog support in Verilator elaborated within the [SV Tools Project](https://github.com/chipsalliance/sv-tools), open source radiation hardening and fault injection in [VeeR](https://github.com/chipsalliance/VeeR) and [Verilator](https://github.com/verilator), and updates on CHIPS Alliance objectives and the newly-formed [OpenROAD Initiative](https://github.com/The-OpenROAD-Project/OpenROAD). [Revisit the following sessions on YouTube](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLBmKb8Xb7kCM&si=N5xVN19pXnlahjF1):

Krzysztof Biegański (Antmicro) presented “Closing in on full SystemVerilog support in Verilator: Four-state, UVM, SVA, and Coverage,” sharing the latest on the ongoing effort to expand Verilator’s verification capabilities, including four-state logic, constrained randomization, UVM, SVA, and coverage.
