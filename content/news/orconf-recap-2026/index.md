CHIPS Alliance was proud to be a Silver Sponsor of Fossi Foundation’s ORConf 2026, held September 11–13 in Ghent, Belgium.

ORConf is a weekend of presentations and networking dedicated to free and open source silicon. CHIPS Alliance Platinum Member Antmicro presented several CHIPS Alliance-related sessions covering SystemVerilog support in Verilator, open source radiation hardening and fault injection in VeeR and Verilator, and updates on CHIPS Alliance objectives and the newly-formed OpenROAD initiative. Revisit the following sessions on YouTube:

Krzysztof Biegański (Antmicro) presented “Closing in on full SystemVerilog support in Verilator: Four-state, UVM, SVA, and Coverage,” sharing the latest on the ongoing effort to expand Verilator’s verification capabilities, including four-state logic, constrained randomization, UVM, SVA, and coverage.
