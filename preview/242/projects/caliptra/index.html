Caliptra

The Caliptra project focuses on development of HW and SW IP for the Caliptra Root of Trust

IP and firmware for an integrated Root of Trust block

Caliptra targets datacenter-class SoCs such as CPUs, GPUs, DPUs, and TPUs. It includes the specification, silicon logic, ROM, and firmware for implementing a Root of Trust for Measurement (RTM) block inside an SoC. A Caliptra integration provides the SoC with Identity, Measured Boot, and Attestation capabilities.
